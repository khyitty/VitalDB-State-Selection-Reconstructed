    \caption{Exact feed-forward architecture of the PPO controller used in all
    four configurations. The controller receives an already constructed S0
    (34-dimensional) or S1 (42-dimensional) state vector. A parameter-free
    flattening extractor feeds separate one-hidden-layer actor and critic
    networks, each with 128 Tanh units. The actor parameterizes a
    one-dimensional diagonal-Gaussian propofol policy with a learned,
    state-independent log standard deviation; the critic returns a scalar
    state-value estimate. The architecture was confirmed from both the
    implementation and the four serialized final models.}
